# CHK-3334 — Update Logic around Billing Address & Billing Phone Number at Payment Screen

**Type:** Story · **Project:** Checkout · **Status:** To Do · **Parent:** FreedomPay 3DS & Stepper Flow
**Use as:** ✏️ a Product story to sharpen. Real, requirement-shaped AC — convert it to testable Given/When/Then and the gaps fall out.

## Description
As part of the FreedomPay 3DS project, the guest must provide the (billing) phone number linked to the card.

**Current state:** when a logged-in guest enters a billing address different from their profile, we send it to RTP as the *primary* address, overwriting their profile. Risk: someone paying with another person's card overwrites their own primary address.

**Proposed future state:** let the guest either just use the entered address for billing, or also save it as their primary address.

## Acceptance Criteria (as written — sharpen this)
1. Within the Billing Address form for ALL payment gateways, a new checkbox is introduced at the bottom, with new language string.
   1. Default string: "Save this as my primary address"
   2. Checkbox selected by default
   3. Checkbox always displays, whether or not an address is pre-populated
2. When "Save this as my primary address" is selected, the entered address is saved:
   1. To the AW DB as billing address with phone number (if phone required — FP 3DS only)
   2. Sent to RTP as primary address profile and primary phone profile (if phone required — FP 3DS only)
3. When it is NOT selected, the entered address is saved:
   1. To the AW DB as billing address with phone number (if phone required — FP 3DS only)
   2. Nothing sent to RTP
4. Regression: Billing Address in Account > Trips & Orders > Order Details reflects whatever was entered at checkout, regardless of the primary choice.

## Testing Notes (hint at what the AC should cover)

| Guest state | Behaviour | Expected |
|---|---|---|
| Has address & phone profile | Doesn't change billing | Nothing sent to DB or RTP |
| Has address & phone profile | Changes billing, unticks "primary" | Save new billing in AW; no RTP update |
| Has address & phone profile | Changes billing, leaves "primary" ticked | Save in AW; send RTP new primary address + phone |
| No/partial profile | Enters billing, leaves "primary" ticked | Save in AW; send RTP new primary address + phone |
| No/partial profile | Enters billing, unticks "primary" | Save in AW; no RTP update |
