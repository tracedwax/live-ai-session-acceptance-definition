# Module 4: Debrief & Homework

> **Time:** ~8 minutes, in the core. The homework gets handed out here so everyone leaves with it, even if they don't stay for office hours.

## Quick debrief

Two or three rooms share, 60 seconds each. Answer whichever of these you have something real for:

- **What is your skill doing?**
- **What is one thing you learned while writing it?**
- **Did you write it by agent? How do you feel that impacted the work?**
- **Where did Tessl (or the agent review) actually change the skill?**

The point is not a perfect skill in 22 minutes. The point is the loop: state intent, build, review, fix the weakest thing. That loop is the skill you're really learning.

Deeper discussion and iteration continue in [Office Hours](office-hours.md) for anyone who can stay. It's optional.

## Homework

Due before Class 1a (Week 1a, Tuesday):

1. **Use a skill you wrote on real work this week, at least twice.** Not a demo. An actual ticket or artifact. The one from your breakout counts; so does any other skill you write between now and Class 1a.
2. **Post one learning, win, or fail.** Any of the three. A fail is more useful to everyone than silence. Fire-and-forget, no write-up needed. *(Where to post: TBD; we'll confirm before the session.)*
3. **Bring an OpenSpec change you care about** to Class 1a. In progress is fine; it's the material for the next session.
4. **Complete the weekly survey.**

> If your skill turned out to be the wrong one to build, that's a finding. Bring that instead.

## Next session: Class 1a

**Intent engineering, plan modes, and spec-driven development.** Class 1 was about teaching the agent who you are (CLAUDE.md) and what you do repeatedly (skills). Class 1a goes one level up: **intent engineering**, in Nate B. Jones's sense, making the business's purpose machine-actionable so the agent stays on the rails. Goals, values, tradeoffs, decision boundaries written down well enough that the agent doesn't have to guess what "good" means here. Plan modes and spec-driven dev are the implementation surface on top of that. Bring the OpenSpec change from homework item 4; we work on real ones.

## Before you drop off

Two quick drops in chat:

1. **One thing from this session you want to do in your work this week.** Naming it out loud (or in chat) is the difference between "neat" and "I'll actually do that."
2. Your **0-4 return-on-time rating** and the one thing that would move it up a point.

Both directly shape Class 1a.

Thanks for the hour. See you in office hours or at Class 1a.
