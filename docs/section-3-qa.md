# Section 3 — Back together: show & ask

> **Time: ~8 min. All together.** Pull both rooms back. The point is to see that the *same loop* produced two different useful things, and to clear whatever got fuzzy.

## Show two results (4 min)

One Product, one QA. ~90 seconds each, screen shared. Ask the person to answer just one of these:

- **What does your ticket look like now vs. when you started?**
- **Where did `grill me` change your mind** — what did it catch that you'd have shipped?
- **Did you push to Jira / save the file? How did the confirm-before-push feel?**

Keep it concrete. "Here's the before, here's the after" beats any explanation.

## Open Q&A (4 min)

Anything from the hour. Likely ones, with the short answer:

- **"When do I trust the push?"** — When you've read the draft and you'd have written something close to it yourself. The review *is* the safety.
- **"What if I don't have a good example to paste?"** — Use the [example tickets](example-tickets.md). PUR-6243 is the one to copy for shape.
- **"Is this just for AC?"** — No. Same loop works for the whole story, a test plan, a release note. AC is just today's rep.
- **"CLAUDE.md vs. a skill — which?"** — A `CLAUDE.md` is a preference Claude always loads in that folder. A skill is the packaged, one-command version you can reuse anywhere. Start with the preference; graduate to the skill.

> **If the room is quiet:** ask *"what almost stopped you?"* — the friction answers are the most useful for next week.
